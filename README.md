# 🌦️ Mood Weather

> A visual emotional wellness journal that turns your daily moods into personal weather.

## ✨ About

Mood Weather is a lightweight emotional wellness web application designed to make self-reflection simple and intuitive.

Instead of presenting emotions only through numbers, charts, or traditional mood scales, Mood Weather represents them as weather. A positive day might feel sunny, a mixed day partly cloudy, and a difficult day stormy.

As users check in over time, their individual moods form a 7-day emotional weather history. The application uses these check-ins to provide pattern insights, a transparent personal forecast, and simple consistency and streak tracking.

The goal isn't to tell users how they should feel. It is to help them notice how they've been feeling.

## 💡 Inspiration

We often check the weather to understand what kind of day we're going to have, but we rarely pause to check in with ourselves in the same way.

We wanted to create a wellness experience that felt less clinical and more intuitive. Instead of asking users to interpret graphs and numbers, we wanted to give emotional patterns a visual language that people already understand.

That idea became Mood Weather.

## 🎯 Problem

Emotional self-reflection can feel like another task to complete. Traditional mood trackers can also rely heavily on numbers, charts, and text, which may make the experience feel clinical or repetitive.

There is value in helping people notice changes in their emotional patterns without turning the experience into diagnosis or judgment.

## 🌦️ Our Solution

Mood Weather turns a quick daily mood check-in into an emotional weather experience.

Users can:

1. Check in with their current mood.
2. See that mood represented as weather.
3. Build a 7-day emotional weather history.
4. Explore patterns in their recent check-ins.
5. View a transparent personal forecast based on their recorded patterns.
6. Track their check-in consistency and streak.

The weather metaphor connects individual emotional states with a larger picture of how the user's week has been feeling.

## ✨ Features

### 🌤️ Mood Check-in

Users can quickly record how they are feeling using simple mood/weather states, with an optional reflection.

### 🌦️ Emotional Weather

Each mood is translated into a visual weather state, creating a consistent language throughout the application.

### 📅 7-Day Emotional Weather History

Recent check-ins are displayed as a seven-day weather timeline, making emotional changes easier to notice.

### 🔍 Pattern Insights

Mood Weather analyzes recent check-ins to surface simple patterns in the user's emotional history.

### 🌤️ Transparent Forecast

The forecast is based on the user's recorded mood patterns rather than presenting an unexplained prediction.

The goal is pattern awareness, not medical prediction.

### 🔥 Streak & Consistency

Users can see their current check-in streak and how consistently they have checked in during the last seven days.

### ✨ Demo Mode

Demo Mode provides realistic sample data so that the complete experience can be explored immediately without requiring a week of previous check-ins.

### ♿ Accessibility

The interface includes semantic labels, keyboard focus states, screen-reader-friendly controls, decorative animation handling, reduced-motion support, and responsive layouts.

### 📱 Responsive Design

The application is designed to work across desktop, tablet, and smaller screen sizes.

## ⚙️ How It Works

The application follows a simple flow:

Mood Check-in
→ Mood-to-Weather Mapping
→ Saved Emotional Data
→ 7-Day History
→ Pattern Analysis
→ Transparent Forecast

User check-ins are stored locally and used to generate the dashboard's emotional weather, history, insights, streak, consistency, and forecast.
